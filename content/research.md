My research uses high-precision ion-trap mass spectrometry to answer questions in neutrino physics, nuclear structure and nuclear astrophysics. The first-author papers below are grouped by theme; the full list is on the [Publications](/publications/) page.

## Exotic N = Z nuclei up to ¹⁰⁰Sn — MASSPASS

Academy Fellowship project (PI). Mass measurements of exotic *N* = *Z* nuclei up to ¹⁰⁰Sn and their vicinity at IGISOL (Jyväskylä) and RIKEN (Japan), probing shell evolution towards the heaviest self-conjugate doubly magic nucleus and the nuclear inputs to the rp-process. [Project page](https://www.jyu.fi/en/projects/mass-measurements-of-exotic-nz-nuclei-up-to-100sn-and-the-vicinity-for-nuclear-physics-and-nuclear).

Beam time as spokesperson: *Mass measurements of N = Z nuclei and the vicinity at the RIBF* (No. 1, 2018; No. 2, 2022, 9 days each, with T. Uesaka).

- **Z. Ge** *et al.*, High-precision mass measurements of neutron-deficient silver isotopes probe the robustness of the *N* = 50 shell closure, [Phys. Rev. Lett. **133**, 132503 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.133.132503)
- **Z. Ge**, Q. Wang, M. Wang, T. Uesaka, Study of mass-measurement method for *N* = *Z* nuclei with isochronous mass spectrometry, [Nucl. Phys. Rev. **36**, 294 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.11804/NuclPhysRev.36.03.294)

## Q-values for neutrino-mass determination

Spokesperson of the IGISOL proposal *Continued search of very low Q-value β and electron-capture decays with JYFLTRAP* (2020, with T. Eronen). Direct Penning-trap measurements of decay energies (Q-values) to identify ultra-low-Q β⁻ and electron-capture transitions for future direct neutrino-mass experiments, and to rule out candidates that do not qualify.

- **Z. Ge** *et al.*, Precise determination of electron-capture Q value of ¹¹³Sn decay related to electron neutrino mass measurements, [Phys. Rev. C **114**, 035501 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1103/bgj9-mzcq) — Q<sub>EC</sub> = 1039.25(19) keV, eight times more precise than AME2020
- **Z. Ge** *et al.*, High-precision direct decay energy measurements of the electron-capture decay of ⁹⁷Tc, [Phys. Rev. C **112**, 035501 (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1103/g393-xx1w) — Q<sub>EC</sub> = 324.82(21) keV

## Storage-ring mass measurements and MCP detectors

PhD work at the RIKEN Nishina Center Rare-RI Ring: secondary-ion identification and selection for isochronous mass measurements, and timing and position-sensitive micro-channel-plate (MCP) detectors. Later work extends to MR-TOF spectrometers at IGISOL and the FRS Ion Catcher at GSI, and detector concepts for HIAF.

- **Z. Ge** *et al.*, Overview of high-performance timing and position-sensitive MCP detectors utilizing secondary electron emission for mass measurements of exotic nuclei at nuclear physics facilities, [Sensors **24**, 7261 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.3390/s24227261)

I am an **Academy Research Fellow and Principal Investigator** at the [Department of Physics](https://www.jyu.fi/en/people/zhuang-ge), Faculty of Mathematics and Science, University of Jyväskylä, Finland. My field is **nuclear physics and nuclear astrophysics**: I measure the masses, Q-values and decays of exotic nuclei with ion traps.

I work in the Exotic Nuclei and Beams (IGISOL) group, using Penning-trap (JYFLTRAP) and multi-reflection time-of-flight (MR-TOF) mass spectrometry. As PI of the Academy Fellowship project [MASSPASS](https://www.jyu.fi/en/projects/mass-measurements-of-exotic-nz-nuclei-up-to-100sn-and-the-vicinity-for-nuclear-physics-and-nuclear), I lead mass measurements of exotic *N* = *Z* nuclei up to doubly magic ¹⁰⁰Sn at IGISOL and RIKEN.

I hold two PhDs: from Saitama University and RIKEN Nishina Center in Japan (2018), on a foil-MCP detector for mass measurements at the Rare-RI Ring, and from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences / Institute of Modern Physics (2019), on high-resolution beam-line systems and mass measurements of *N* = *Z* nuclei. I have worked at all three radioactive-ion-beam facilities with storage rings (RIKEN, GSI and IMP) and at the IGISOL ISOL facility, as a postdoctoral researcher at JYFLTRAP (2019–2021) and in the FRS/Super-FRS group at GSI (2021–2023), before returning to Jyväskylä as Academy Research Fellow in 2023.

My group currently includes two postdocs and a PhD student, and I teach the PhD course *Lasers and Traps in Nuclear Physics Studies*. I have co-authored more than 90 peer-reviewed papers, including in *Nature*, *Physical Review Letters*, *Physics Letters B* and *The Astrophysical Journal*.

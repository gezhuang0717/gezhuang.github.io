## Exotic N = Z nuclei up to ¹⁰⁰Sn — MASSPASS

Academy Fellowship project (PI). Mass measurements of exotic *N* = *Z* nuclei up to ¹⁰⁰Sn and their vicinity at IGISOL (Jyväskylä) and RIKEN (Japan), probing shell evolution towards the heaviest self-conjugate doubly magic nucleus and the nuclear physics input to the rp-process. [Project page](https://www.jyu.fi/en/projects/mass-measurements-of-exotic-nz-nuclei-up-to-100sn-and-the-vicinity-for-nuclear-physics-and-nuclear)

## Q-values for neutrino-mass determination

Direct Penning-trap measurements of decay energies (Q-values) identify ultra-low-Q β⁻ and electron-capture transitions for future direct neutrino-mass experiments, and rule out candidates that do not qualify.

## Storage rings, MR-TOF and MCP detectors

PhD work at the RIKEN Rare-RI Ring: secondary-ion identification for isochronous mass measurements and timing / position-sensitive micro-channel-plate (MCP) detectors. Later work extends to MR-TOF spectrometers at IGISOL and the FRS Ion Catcher at GSI, and detector concepts for HIAF.

## Ion-optics design

Ion-optical design of beam lines, storage rings and trap set-ups: the injection ion optics of the RIKEN Rare-RI Ring, simulation studies of isochronous mass measurements, and today RFQ, MR-TOF and Penning-trap beam-line design with SIMION.

- Z. Ge, Q. Wang, M. Wang, T. Uesaka, Study of mass-measurement method for *N* = *Z* nuclei with isochronous mass spectrometry, [Nucl. Phys. Rev. 36, 294 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.11804/NuclPhysRev.36.03.294)
- Z. Ge *et al.*, Online results for the injection ion optics of the Rare RI Ring, [RIKEN Accel. Prog. Rep. 49, 180 (2016)](https://www.nishina.riken.jp/researcher/APR/APR049/pdf/180.pdf)

## Nuclear physics

- Shell evolution and magic numbers far from stability — *N* = *Z* nuclei towards ¹⁰⁰Sn
- Isospin symmetry, mirror nuclei and Coulomb displacement energies
- Separation energies, pairing and isomers from precise masses
- Q-values of rare decays for neutrino physics

## Nuclear astrophysics

- Masses at rp- and νp-process waiting points near *N* = *Z*
- Neutron-rich masses as input for r-process network calculations
- Nuclear input for X-ray bursts and the origin of the elements
